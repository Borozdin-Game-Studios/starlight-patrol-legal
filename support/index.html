Starlight Patrol: support and feedback

Published by Timofei Borozdin.

Found a bug, stuck somewhere, or have an idea? Write to timofei.borozdin.dev@gmail.com. The same address takes feedback. The game's About & Legal screen has a button that starts this email for you.

What helps

  • The game version (shown on the About & Legal screen) and your phone or tablet model.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened. A screenshot helps.

Please don't send passwords or other private information; we never need them. How we handle your email is described in the privacy policy.

Your game progress

Progress is saved only on your device. To start over, clear the app's storage in your phone's settings (Settings → Apps → Starlight Patrol → Storage → Clear data) or uninstall the game. We can't restore lost progress: we never receive it.
