Starlight Patrol privacy policy

Effective 19 November 2026 · applies to the Starlight Patrol app for Android (com.timofeiborozdin.starlightpatrol), version 1.0 and later.

Who we are

Starlight Patrol is published by Timofei Borozdin, an individual entrepreneur registered in Georgia (“we”, “us”). We are responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: timofei.borozdin.dev@gmail.com. Our contact details are also shown on the game's Google Play page.

In short

  • The game has no accounts, advertising, in-app purchases, analytics, tracking or multiplayer.
  • It needs no internet connection and asks for no device permissions.
  • The app does not send any data to us or to anyone else.
  • If you email us, we receive what you choose to send, and use it only to reply.

Data kept on your device

To save your progress, the game stores a small file in its private app storage on your device. It contains:

WhatWhy
Game progress: credits, purchased ship upgrades, completed contracts, outpost status, whether training was doneSo you can continue where you left off
Settings: sound, music and effect volume, aim assist level, steering sensitivity, text size, high-contrast markersSo the game keeps your preferences
Internal record numbers of completed rewardsSo a reward is never paid twice

This file contains no name, contact details, location, device identifiers or advertising IDs. It stays on your device; we never receive it and cannot see it.

Backups and moving to a new device

The game turns off Android cloud backup for its data, so your progress is not uploaded to your Google account backup. On Android 12 and later, if you use Android's own device-to-device transfer when you set up a new phone, Android may copy the game's data to the new device. That transfer is done by Android, not by us, and we receive nothing.

Deleting your data

You are in control of the save file. Clear it at any time in your device's settings (Settings → Apps → Starlight Patrol → Storage → Clear data), or uninstall the game; either deletes it from that device. A copy made by a device-to-device transfer stays on the device it was copied to until you clear or uninstall the game there.

When you contact us

Support and feedback are by email. When you write to us we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach, through our email provider (Google Gmail). We use it only to answer you and to fix the problems you report. We do not sell it, use it for marketing or share it, except where the law requires us to. We keep support emails for up to 12 months after the conversation ends, then delete them. You can ask us at any time to delete your messages sooner, or to tell you what we hold; write to the address above. Our legal basis for handling your email is our legitimate interest in answering you (and, where you request it, the steps you ask us to take).

Google Play

You download the game through Google Play. Google processes information for that (such as your account and purchase history, crash and performance reports if you have chosen to share them with developers, and reviews you post) under Google's privacy policy. The game itself adds nothing to this.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal data from anyone, of any age, through the app.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or Georgia), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to your data protection authority. For this game the only such data is any email you have sent us. Write to us and we will answer within one month.

Security

The save file is kept in the game's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Support email is kept in a password-protected mailbox.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes how it handles data (for example by adding a new feature that needs it), we will update this page first and change the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the game's release notes.
